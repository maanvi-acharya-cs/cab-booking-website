RideBuddy Reserve – Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to the RideBuddy “Reserve” (advance booking) feature. Please check the app for the latest city-specific rules and updates.

1. Scheduling

  • You can schedule your pickup up to 90 days in advance.
  • Your driver will be assigned closer to the pickup time.

2. Wait Time

  • Additional wait time is included at pickup (duration varies by city and ride type).
  • After the included wait time, no-show or cancellation fees may apply.

3. Pricing & Fees

  • An estimated fare is shown when booking; the final fare may vary due to traffic, route, or time.
  • Cancellation and no-show fees may apply as per local policies.

4. Changes & Cancellations

  • Changes to pickup time or location are subject to availability.
  • Free cancellation is allowed up to 60 minutes in advance (may vary by city).

5. Availability

  • Reserve may not be available in all cities or for all vehicle types.
  • Extreme weather, traffic, or other conditions may cause delays.

6. Safety

  • Verify driver and vehicle details in the app before starting your trip.
  • Emergency and safety features are available in the app (availability varies by city).
